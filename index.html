About

"Where finance meets technology — building skills at the intersection of both."

I am an incoming BBA Finance student at the Colin Powell School, City College of New York. My background is unconventional — I came up through a technical high school where I built computers, terminated network cables, and wrote code before ever opening a finance textbook.

That combination is deliberate. I believe the future of finance is technical, and I want to be someone who understands both sides. I have completed coursework in tax simulation, accounting careers, and financial literacy through EVERFI, while holding industry certifications in network cabling and workforce competency from NOCTI.

Outside of academics, I write for my school newspaper, volunteer with the Queens Tech Squad, and am building an app to connect people in my community with volunteering and stipend opportunities.
